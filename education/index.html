Seven-Year Scriptural Training

Year Texts Covered
Year 1 – Year 2 (first half) Khordeh Avesta in Gujarati script: Kasti prayers, Din-no-Kalmo, Jamvani Baj, 101 names of God, 30 roz, 12 mah, Hamkaras, Geh, Gahambars, Gathas, Sarosh Baj, Tandarosti, Hoshbam, 5 Geh, Patet Pashemani, 5 Nyaishnas, and more.
Year 2 (second half) Avestan script introduced; Yasna learning begins (up to Ha 10).
Year 3 Yasna Ha 11–34
Year 4 Yasna Ha 35–53
Year 5 Entire Yasna (to Ha 72); Visparad, supplementary texts, Afringans, various Baj
Year 6 Daily priestly rituals: Afringans, Afrins, Farokhshi, Jashan; Vendidad Fragard I–XII
Year 7 Vendidad Fragard XIII–XXII and allied prayers; readiness for Maratab

Dadar Athornan Institute

Established 9th November 1919 by the Athornan Mandal. Providing scriptural, religious, and secular education to children of Parsi priests for over a century.
