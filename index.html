Welcome to the Institute

The Dadar Athornan Institute (formerly the Athornan Boarding Madressa, Dadar) was established as a charitable institution by the Athornan Mandal — an association of Zoroastrian priests — on 9th November 1919.

Its central mission has been to give the children of Parsi priests, especially those from mofussil areas, an opportunity to receive systematic scriptural, ritual, religious, and secular education, along with boarding, lodging, and allied facilities like medicines, books, and stationery — absolutely free of cost.

Situated in the heart of Dadar Parsi Colony in Mumbai, the Institute has been a beacon of Zoroastrian learning for over a century. More than 500 students have passed through its doors since inception, and its alumni include distinguished Dasturs and Ervads who have served the community across India and the world.

In 2019, the Institute completed 100 years of dedicated service to the Zoroastrian community. It continues to light the lamps of devotion and learning in the hearts of young priests and inspire them to tend the holy fires.
