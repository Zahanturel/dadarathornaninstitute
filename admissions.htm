NEW ADMISSIONS
At Athornan Boarding Madressa, Dadar
   
 
New Admissions are taken at Dadar Athornan Instiute every year from June. Admissions forms can be collected from the Institute office in January and submitted by March. The student and parents are called for Admission formalities in the middle of April. Boys belonging to priestly families (Athornans) from Std. I to III are eligible for admission.

Students are provided with complete training for priesthood and academic education (including computer) upto S.S.C. at the D.P.Y.A. High School. Basic knowledge about Zoroastrian religion, Iranian history and Bhantar meaning is also taught.

All of the above, as also boarding, lodging, and other facilities like laundry and medical care, are provided absolutely free of costs, in a homely atmosphere.
