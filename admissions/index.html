Admissions

At Athornan Boarding Madressa, Dadar

New Admissions

At Athornan Boarding Madressa, Dadar

New Admissions are taken at Dadar Athornan Institute every year from June. Admissions forms can be collected from the Institute office in January and submitted by March. The student and parents are called for Admission formalities in the middle of April.

Eligibility

Boys belonging to priestly families (Athornans) from Std. I to III are eligible for admission.

What the Institute Provides

Students are provided with complete training for priesthood and academic education (including computer) upto S.S.C. at the D.P.Y.A. High School. Basic knowledge about Zoroastrian religion, Iranian history and Bhantar meaning is also taught.

📚

Scriptural Training

Complete 7-year training in Khordeh Avesta, Yasna, Visperad, and Vendidad

🏫

Secular Education

Free schooling at D.P.Y.A. High School up to SSC level, with private tuitions

🏠

Boarding & Lodging

Residential accommodation in a homely atmosphere, absolutely free

🩺

Medical Care

Medicare centre and medical expenses covered for all students

👕

Laundry

Laundry facilities provided to all students

💻

Computer Education

Computer education included as part of the academic curriculum

All of the above — absolutely free of cost

Provided in a homely atmosphere, with complete care and mentorship.
