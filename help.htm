HOW CAN YOU HELP THE INSTITUTE?
The Dadar Athornan Institute is a public charitable Institution funded by donations from philanthropic individuals and Trusts. The accounts are duly audited every year by a reputed firm of Accountants.

Today more than ever, with the regularly increasing rate of inflation, the Institute seeks your support and patronage by way of generous donations, which may be made to particular schemes or earmarked funds.
* Sponsoring a student:

On the basis of the expenses that the Institute annually incurs on a student, a student can be sponsored for a year by a donation of Rs. 2,00,0.00 (Rupees Two lacs only).

The donor can also maintain an Endowment Fund by paying a sum of Rs. 20,00, 0000/- (Rupees Twenty lacs only), whereby a student will be educated in the name of the donor’s family for a period of 10 years.

If the sponsorship is in memory of a departed one, the student recites the name of the deceased in regular rituals and prayers. He also prays the Tandarosti of the donor and his/her family.

Part sponsorship of a student, that covers just the education and not the boarding and lodging, is for Rs. 30,000/- per annum.
* Annual Baj-Rojgar Ceremonies Schemes:

Afringan, Farokhshi, Baj and Stum (on fruits) are performed once a year by students for five years on the rojgar (death anniversary day) of a deceased person, for a donation of Rs.50,000/-
* Sponsoring a Meal:

A donor can sponsor one meal for students. On festive occasions, students recite a Tandarosti. On death anniversaries a Stum (on fruits) is recited. The costs are Rs.3,000 for a vegetarian meal, and Rs.4,500 for a non-vegetarian meal. Sweet-dish (pudding, custard, jelly etc.) or ice-cream can be provided for an additional sum of Rs. 1000 or Rs.1500 respectively. Annual bookings of meals can also be made. A breakfast can be sponsored for Rs. 1000, evening snack for Rs. 4000 and an outing (movie, snack and transport) for Rs.15,000. Owing to inflation, the above amount may be revised from time to time.
Other earmarked Funds:
1) General Maintenance Fund: Money can be utilized for any Institute related activity.
2) Food Fund: Utilised towards providing food.
3) Milk Fund: Utilised towards providing milk to students at breakfast.
4) Education Fund: For educational expenses and tuitions fees.
5) Book Fund: Utilised towards purchasing text books and note books.
6) Library Fund: For maintaining the Institute Library and purchasing books for the same
7) Medical Fund: For students’ medical expenses and operating the Medicare Centre.
8) Mobed Amelioration Fund: For providing assistance to practicing and aged Mobeds all over India.
9) Athornan Mandal-WZO Scheme: For providing monthly stipends and related benefits to working Mobeds all over India.
