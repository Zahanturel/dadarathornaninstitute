Support the Institute

Schemes, funds, and how to donate

The Dadar Athornan Institute is a public charitable Institution funded by donations from philanthropic individuals and Trusts. The accounts are duly audited every year by a reputed firm of Accountants.

Today more than ever, with the regularly increasing rate of inflation, the Institute seeks your support and patronage by way of generous donations, which may be made to particular schemes or earmarked funds.

In 2019, the Institute completed 100 years of service to the Community. The Institute continues to light the lamps of devotion and learning in the hearts of young priests and inspire them to tend the holy fires. We look forward to generous support from donors and patrons.

Make a Donation

Your donation directly sustains the education, boarding, and care of young Zoroastrian priests. All donations to Athornan Mandal are eligible for tax benefits.

How to Donate

Ways to Help

Sponsoring a Student

On the basis of the expenses that the Institute annually incurs on a student, a student can be sponsored for a year by a donation of Rs. 2,00,000/- (Rupees Two lacs only).

The donor can also maintain an Endowment Fund by paying a sum of Rs. 20,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty lacs only), whereby a student will be educated in the name of the donor's family for a period of 10 years.

If the sponsorship is in memory of a departed one, the student recites the name of the deceased. They also pray the Tandarosti of the donor and his/her family.

Part sponsorship of a student, that covers just the education and not the boarding and lodging, is for Rs. 50,000/- per annum.

Annual Baj-Rojgar Ceremonies Scheme

Afringan, Farokhshi, Baj and Stum (on fruits) are performed once a year by students for five years on the rojgar (death anniversary day) of a deceased person, for a donation of Rs. 60,000/-.

Sponsoring a Meal

A donor can sponsor one meal for students. On festive occasions, students recite a Tandarosti. On death anniversaries a Stum (on fruits) is recited.

  • Vegetarian meal: Rs. 4,000/-
  • Non-vegetarian meal: Rs. 4,500/-
  • Sweet-dish (pudding, custard, jelly etc.): additional Rs. 1,000/-
  • Ice-cream: additional Rs. 1,500/-
  • Breakfast: Rs. 1,500/-
  • Evening snack: Rs. 4,000/-
  • Outing (movie, snack and transport): Rs. 15,000/-

Annual bookings of meals can also be made. Owing to inflation, the above amounts may be revised from time to time.
