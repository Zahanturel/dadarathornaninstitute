March 2020 onwards
Owing to the rising threat of Corona virus and the precautions that need to be taken, the DPYA School had closed down since around 10th March. The exams of students were also cancelled. 

Students were sent home on 23rd March. Immediately the next day, Lockdown was imposed in the country by the Prime Minister.

All students were promoted, but 2 students of Std. IX who had not fared well in the previous semester had to give a re-test later. All other students were passed and promoted.

On account of the continued Covid-19 restrictions, schools were not opened physically in June and hence our Institute also did not start physically.

15th June, the DPYA High School online classes for all students in the morning from 9.00 am, with varying number of periods for different standards, and hence Tuitions by Institute academic teachers also started online.

From 1st July Bhantar lesson and revision sessions were taken online every day from 5 to 6 pm. 

In November two mini fire extinguishers were gifted for the Institute by WZO. On 9th Dec, The Zoroastrian Charity Funds of Hong Kong, Canton and Macau, through the WZO Trust Funds and the good offices of Mr. Dinshaw Tamboly, gave gifts of Lenovo Laptops to students from Std.
